Multi-Modal Multi-Agent System for Bridge Operation and Maintenance based on Large Language Model

Xiangyun Luo, Yunkai Zhang, Yong Huang, Stephen Wu 等
2026-11-02cs.MA
摘要
Bridges are critical transportation infrastructure, and reliable operation and maintenance (O&M) is essential for traffic safety and long-term structural performance. However, bridge O&M faces key bottlenecks: no unified architecture coordinates heterogeneous resources; model inferences lack clear links to physical-mechanical laws; and experts' tacit knowledge remains difficult to formalize. This study proposes a knowledge-enhanced autonomy formalization methodology and apply it to construct the Bridge O&M Multi-Modal Multi-Agent System (BOMMA), which integrates heterogeneous diagnostic actions, physics-mechanics-informed representations, long-term memory, and structured experiential knowledge within a coordinated multi-agent workflow, enabling interpretable and traceable scheduling, analysis, and decision-making. On 3,492 multi-source instructions, the proposed method achieves scheduling recalls of 85.37% for simple and 99.40% for composite tasks, with 95.35% BERT F1-score for O&M action reasoning. Ablation studies confirm the contributions of the components in the autonomy. This study bridges general LLM reasoning and specialized bridge O&M requirements.

Safety4DM: A Vision-Language Framework for Risk-Aware Dynamic Maps in Vehicle-Road-Cloud Collaborative Driving

Zhengshu Zhou, Shaoyu Wang, Lu Tao, Xinhui Hu 等
2026-11-01cs.AI
摘要
Dynamic map (DM) is a core information infrastructure for vehicle-road-cloud (VRC) cooperative autonomous driving. It fuses perception data from multiple sources to overcome the inherent line-of-sight and field-of-view limits of single-vehicle perception. However, DM still falls short on driving safety in two ways. First, DM data specifications already define road conditions semantically, but no pipeline yet turns real-world road observations into fine-grained risk entities in the DM. Second, existing DM systems lack a natural language interface for driving risks. To close these gaps, we propose Safety4DM, a safety-oriented language system for VRC dynamic maps. We first build RoadRisk, a real-world road image dataset that covers four types of road conditions and four risk levels. On this dataset, we fine-tune a vision-language model (VLM) for road safety risk perception. The model outputs structured risk representations that the DM can ingest directly. We then introduce a Safety-aware Step-wise Prompting (SafeStep) mechanism as the natural language query interface of the DM. SafeStep answers queries accurately and also generates actionable driving advice. To build and evaluate this interface, we develop SafeVRC-Sim, a VRC cooperative simulation framework, and SafeDM-QA, a safety question-answering benchmark. Experiments show that even the compact Qwen3-VL-4B reaches 89.83% accuracy on risk perception after fine-tuning. SafeStep also plugs easily into multiple LLM families. With medium and large models (20B and above) from five families, it achieves over 93% natural language query accuracy in every case, which shows strong generalization.

Optimization-based lane-changing trajectory planning in mixed traffic with embedded input convex neural networks

Yuanxiang Yang, Yu Liu, Fabricio Oliveira, Yu Han 等
2026-10-28math.OCcs.LG
摘要
Lane-changing (LC) maneuvers are highly challenging vehicle interactions, with substantial implications for traffic safety and efficiency. Developing effective LC strategies for connected and automated vehicles in mixed traffic is complicated by the uncertainty associated with surrounding-vehicle behavior and the computational burden of embedding realistic prediction models within optimization schemes. To capture these complex interactions while retaining tractability, this study proposes a data-driven optimization framework that embeds neural network (NN) surrogates for safety and lateral comfort within a Bézier-curve-based trajectory planner. Specifically, the safety cost during the LC process is quantified via a surrogate composed of an input convex recurrent neural network (ICRNN) encoding the planned trajectory, a fully input convex neural network (FICNN) embedding the LC duration, and a partially input convex neural network (PICNN) decoder fusing them with historical traffic states; separately, another FICNN surrogate approximates the optimal lateral comfort cost as a function of the LC duration. Both surrogates are embedded via linear programming (LP) formulations, avoiding the binary variables required by conventional mixed-integer programming (MIP) approaches for NN-embedded optimization. We further theoretically establish their exactness at optimality and extend the framework to problems with additional analytical objective terms and multiple surrogates. Experiments on a hybrid dataset combining real-world and simulated trajectories demonstrate that the proposed method achieves surrogate accuracy competitive with unconstrained baselines while substantially improving optimization efficiency over conventional MIPs. Closed-loop simulations further show that the proposed planners markedly reduce the weighted planning cost and consistently outperform baseline methods.

RadarCom: Unlocking Simultaneous Communication and Sensing on Commodity Automotive mmWave Radars

Yi Wei, Yue Zhang, Peijun Hou, Qiuyu Liao 等
2026-10-27cs.NI
摘要
Vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication can improve road safety and traffic efficiency, yet its deployment remains limited by the need for dedicated communication hardware and supporting infrastructure. Meanwhile, production vehicles are already widely equipped with millimeter-wave radars for sensing. To unlock their communication potential, this paper presents RadarCom, a software-defined system that enables direct radar-to-radar communication on unmodified commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) automotive mmWave radars while preserving their native sensing functionality. The key insight is that mutual inter-radar interference, traditionally viewed as harmful, can be repurposed as a communication carrier. Based on this insight, RadarCom introduces slope-mismatch-based discovery, adaptive chirp synchronization, differential coding on range-FFT, and sensing-aware interference suppression to enable simultaneous communication and sensing without hardware modification, waveform redesign, or infrastructure support. We implement RadarCom on TI AWR2243 automotive radars and evaluate it under static, dynamic, and challenging real-world conditions. Results show an average effective data rate of 5.55 kbps with symbol error rates below 1.5%, while maintaining sensing F1 scores of up to 80.5% during concurrent communication. These results demonstrate the feasibility of transforming commodity automotive radars into dual-purpose communication and sensing devices.

Uncertainty-Aware Coordinated Multi-Ramp Metering Based on an Action-Conditioned Traffic Prediction Operator Under Sparse Observations

Hongxin Yu, Fengyue Jin, Tao Jiang, Yibing Wang 等
2026-10-31physics.app-ph
摘要
Coordinated multi-ramp metering (CRM) is an important approach to mitigating freeway congestion, but sparse observations in practice complicate the state estimation and model calibration required by predictive control. This study develops an Action-Conditioned Traffic Prediction Operator (ATPO) driven by sparse, heterogeneous observations and candidate multi-ramp actions, and embeds it in an uncertainty-aware CRM framework (ATPOUCRM). ATPO forms a four-channel history from the density and speed measurements of fixed detectors and probe vehicles and their separate observation masks. Queries at future spatiotemporal points attend to all valid historical observations before incorporating upstream mainline demand and on-ramp queue information. Candidate metering actions condition the latent field through feature-wise linear modulation, a spatial Fourier operator, and a causal temporal kernel. The operator produces action-conditioned predictive distributions of future density and speed throughout the corridor; a bootstrap ensemble additionally characterizes disagreement among models. Using these predictions, ATPOUCRM primarily minimizes expected total time spent, including mainline travel and on-ramp waiting, while penalizing predictive uncertainty near merge areas. It uses the cross-entropy method to search candidate actions and applies a safety filter before execution. For five-minute global traffic state prediction on a simulated 7-km freeway corridor, ATPO achieves root mean square errors of 2.54 veh/km/lane, 4.56 km/h, and 0.76 veh*h for density, speed, and total time spent over the forecast horizon, respectively, outperforming the comparison methods. Across 100 control scenarios, ATPOUCRM reduces mean total time spent by 26.17% relative to no control. ATPO also generates predictions on finer spatiotemporal grids without retraining.

GAS-PFN: Graph-Aware Spatiotemporal Prior-Data Fitted Networks for Zero-Shot Traffic Forecasting

Joseph Natter, Long Vu, Yifan Zhang, Bobin Deng 等
2026-10-27cs.LGcs.AIcs.NE
摘要
Traffic forecasting requires modeling complex temporal dynamics and the spatial dependencies imposed by road topology. Supervised graph-based models achieve strong performance, but are often trained for a particular dataset and sensor graph, making deployment to new or evolving networks costly. Prior-Data Fitted Networks (PFNs) offer a zero-shot alternative by learning in-context inference from synthetic task priors, which provide an explicit mechanism for encoding domain-specific assumptions. Existing forecasting PFNs, however, do not explicitly represent the graph-structured dependencies governing traffic dynamics. To combine graph-aware traffic modeling with zero-shot adaptation, we introduce the Graph-Aware Spatiotemporal Prior-Data Fitted Network (GAS-PFN). GAS-PFN incorporates sampled road topology into its synthetic task prior through random-walk diffusion, teaching the model to learn graph-coupled dynamics during pretraining. Its co-designed inference architecture uses two mask-conditioned passes through a shared Transformer backbone to separately model sensor-specific temporal dynamics and spatiotemporal interactions among neighboring sensors before integrating their representations for prediction. This design enables GAS-PFN to use target-network structure and context without parameter updates. Across six benchmarks at the 90-minute horizon, GAS-PFN reduces MSE by 34.0% on average relative to TimePFN, its closest PFN-based predecessor. It also reduces MSE by 27.7% on average relative to four recent industry-developed general-purpose zero-shot forecasters. In this comparison, all models receive 90 minutes of contiguous recent history, and any additional context is drawn from each dataset's chronological training split. These results indicate that aligning PFN priors and inference architectures with graph structure improves in-context learning for zero-shot traffic forecasting.

Effects of Competing Reactions on $^{59}\mathrm{Cu}$ and $^{63}\mathrm{Ga}$ on Abundance Distributions in Type I X-Ray Bursts

Yinwen Guan, Ertao Li, Zhihong Li, Youbao Wang 等
2026-11-01astro-ph.HEnucl-th
摘要
The composition of X-ray burst ashes affects the thermal transport properties of the neutron star crust, as well as heating and cooling through weak interaction processes. Reaction-rate uncertainties can produce secondary peaks separated from the main peak in final abundance distributions. Using STARLIB reaction rates and their temperature-dependent uncertainties in the K04 one-zone post-processing model, we combine four sets of Monte Carlo calculations with XGBoost classification, SHAP analysis, and reaction-flow analysis to investigate the origins of these secondary peaks. We find that the competing $(p,α)$ and $(p,γ)$ reaction pairs on $^{59}\mathrm{Cu}$ and $^{63}\mathrm{Ga}$ are primarily responsible for secondary peaks outside the central 68\% confidence interval in all 44 isotopes examined. Fixing both pairs at their median rates reduces the fractions of samples in the secondary-peak regions by 96\%--100\%, while the bounds of the central 68\% interval remain largely unchanged for most mass numbers. The $^{59}\mathrm{Cu}$ pair has the larger effect. Enhanced recycling limits the supply of material to heavier isotopes and accelerates proton consumption, while earlier proton exhaustion further suppresses subsequent proton captures. These changes alter the balance between isotope production and destruction, leading to high- or low-abundance secondary peaks over a broad mass range beyond the Ni--Cu and Zn--Ga cycles. These results identify $^{59}\mathrm{Cu}(p,γ)^{60}\mathrm{Zn}$ and the two competing channels on $^{63}\mathrm{Ga}$ as priorities for further nuclear-physics constraints to improve XRB abundance predictions.

Local Spatial TreeSHAP: Geographically Explainable AI Revealing Urbanization-Dependent Spatial Heterogeneity in Flood Resilience

Zeyu Zhao, Xiaoshan Zhou, Fanxin Meng, Dongping Fang 等
2026-10-30cs.LG
摘要
Urban flood resilience emerges from complex interactions among natural, built, and socio-economic systems, yet most recovery policies implicitly assume that resilience mechanisms are spatially uniform. To address this gap, this study develops a spatially explainable machine learning framework that integrates geographically weighted random forests with a novel Local Spatial TreeSHAP algorithm, enabling nonlinear resilience mechanisms to be identified and interpreted at the community scale. The framework is applied to 779 communities affected by the July 2023 extreme rainstorm in Beijing. Results reveal that resilience mechanisms exhibit substantially stronger spatial heterogeneity than the underlying physical and socio-economic conditions themselves, with SHAP-value spatial autocorrelation decreasing by 66.2% on average relative to predictor variables. Three distinct resilience regimes emerge across the urbanization gradient. Highly urbanized communities benefit from synergistic interactions among commercial infrastructure and accommodation services; transitional medium urbanized communities are constrained by infrastructure-development mismatches that create facility gaps despite continued urban expansion; and low urbanized communities are dominated by ecological and topographic thresholds, where adverse interactions between transportation and river-network structures undermine recovery capacity. Field investigations and interviews with 12 stakeholders corroborate these patterns. By linking geographically weighted machine learning with locally grounded attribution analysis, the proposed framework provides a new pathway for uncovering spatially varying resilience mechanisms and translating them into place-based recovery strategies under increasing climate extremes.
